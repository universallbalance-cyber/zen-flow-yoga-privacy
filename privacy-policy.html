Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Thank you for using Zen Flow Yoga and Meditate ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

Zen Flow Yoga and Meditate is designed with your privacy in mind. The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or contact information.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

All data you create within the App — including journal entries, saved sequences, and meditation preferences — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

  • Journal entries — stored locally, never transmitted.
  • Yoga sequences — saved on your device only.
  • Meditation timer settings — stored locally.

If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data will be permanently deleted from your device.

3. Yoga Pose Images

All yoga pose images displayed in the App are stored locally within the App itself. No images are fetched from external servers during normal use.

4. Sharing Features

The App includes an optional feature that allows you to share your yoga sequences with others using your device's native share functionality. This sharing is entirely optional and initiated only by you. We do not have access to what you share or with whom.

5. Children's Privacy

Zen Flow Yoga and Meditate is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take steps to remove such information.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
